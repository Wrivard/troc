# Public Marketplace Pages

## `/`
Marketplace immediately, not startup landing page.

Header:
- TROC logo;
- Shop;
- Sell;
- Collect;
- dominant search;
- EN/FR;
- theme;
- account/sign in;
- cart.

Sections:
- Trending Today;
- popular/new sets;
- deals;
- recently sold activity;
- Complete Your Collection / Smart Cart;
- featured Canadian sellers/LGS;
- Canada-first value proposition;
- Founding Seller CTA in prelaunch/demo environment.

## `/search`
Canonical product results only.

## `/games/[slug]`
Sets, trending, singles/sealed and filters.

## `/sets/[slug]`
Set information, visual/checklist browsing, reference values, filters, owned/missing status, Find Missing Cards.

## `/product/[slug]`
Canonical image, game/set, number, rarity, printing/variant, language selector, Reference Price, Lowest Available, Median Available, price history, want count.

Seller offers show seller, verification, reputation, condition, price, quantity, shipping estimate, seller minimum context, promotions and Add to Cart.

Photo/graded offers are item-specific.

Actions:
- wishlist;
- want list;
- price alert;
- add to collection;
- catalog correction.

## `/store/[sellerSlug]`
Major differentiator.

Header:
- logo/banner;
- seller name;
- city/province;
- badges;
- feedback;
- demo sales count;
- followers.

Tabs:
- Shop;
- Deals;
- About;
- Reviews.

Features:
- search this seller;
- game/set filters;
- wishlist matches;
- missing master-set matches;
- cards under $1;
- new arrivals;
- promotion/free-shipping progress.

Verified LGS may show address, hours and website/social links.

## SEO
Indexable bilingual game, set, product, sealed and seller pages with canonical URLs, hreflang, useful metadata and sitemap strategy.
