# Canada-First Shipping

Standardized shipping classes are essential for low-value singles.

## Classes
Configurable rules may use packed weight, thickness, item/card count, product type, value and tracking requirements.

Do not hard-code current postal prices into domain logic.

## Seller setup
Handling time, supported classes/services, tracked options and eligible free-shipping threshold.

## Combined shipping
Calculate from aggregate Seller Order, not per listing.

## Level gating
Entry level cannot enable platform free shipping.
Higher levels can unlock it via configurable entitlements.

## Tracking
Threshold configurable; initial planning value around CAD $50.

## Canada Post
Future only: rates, label purchase, tracking update.
Create ShippingProvider now; no real label purchase in MVP.
