# Compliance Readiness, Analytics & Notifications

This is product architecture, not final legal advice.

## Compliance readiness
Prepare fields/workflows for seller identity, business type, tax registration, payout/KYC status and reporting periods.
Restrict sensitive fields.

## Privacy/consent
Consent records, preferences, access/deletion workflow placeholders, retention awareness and minimal collection.

## Marketing
Preserve newsletter/waitlist consent evidence and unsubscribe state.

## Draft legal routes
Terms, Privacy, Seller Agreement, Purchase Protection, Condition Guide, Prohibited Items, Returns/Issues.
Do not claim they are lawyer-approved.

## Analytics events
Search, product view, add to cart, seller-minimum progress, Smart Cart, checkout, cards/order, sellers/order, wishlist, collection completion, Find Missing Cards, seller follow, price alert, seller application and CSV import.

## Notifications
Orders, shipping, messages, offers, price alerts, wishlist availability, followed-seller restock/promotion, review reminders, seller approval/level milestones and catalog corrections.
