# Trust, Reviews, Messaging & Condition

## Reviews
Buyer → seller only.
1–5 stars + comment + positive-feedback percentage.
Configurable auto-positive feedback after completion if buyer does not review.

## Messaging
Order/listing-linked conversations with text and safe image attachments plus report action.

## Condition guide
Official bilingual NM / LP / MP / HP / DMG visual guide.
Photos supplement but do not erase condition standard.

MVP issue categories:
- wrong card;
- materially incorrect condition;
- counterfeit concern;
- missing card;
- damaged shipment;
- item not received.

Keep dispute management light/admin-supported.

## Graded
Store grading company, grade, cert number and photos.
Use configurable grader catalog.
