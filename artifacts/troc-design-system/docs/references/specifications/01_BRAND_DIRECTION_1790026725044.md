# 01 — Brand Direction

## Brand
TROC

## Descriptor
Canada's Trading Card Marketplace
French equivalent should be concise and natural, e.g. marketplace copy localized appropriately rather than awkward literal translation.

## Primary campaign line
Built here. For collectors here.

## Supporting lines
- Collect. Trade. Belong.
- More cards. Less shipping.
- Sell cards. Keep more.
- One search. Every seller.

## Personality
- Canadian
- confident
- fast
- precise
- premium
- collector-aware
- technology-forward
- not corporate-boring
- not childish/gamer-cliché

## Visual language
The brand should feel like a serious modern marketplace that happens to specialize in trading cards. The UI is restrained so card art can be visually rich.

## Logo
The selected wordmark is the italic, forward-moving TROC reference in `assets/selected-wordmark-reference.png`.

The final identity pairs the wordmark with a custom red TROC maple-leaf symbol. The leaf shown in the reference board is directional only. Do NOT trace/recreate the official Canadian flag leaf and do NOT finalize the leaf without a supplied vector asset later.

For this milestone, use a temporary clean custom placeholder leaf that is clearly marked internally as provisional, or use the supplied raster reference only where necessary.

## Canadian identity
Strong in language and positioning, restrained in decoration. Avoid repeated maple-leaf wallpaper, flags everywhere, clichés, hockey motifs, etc.
