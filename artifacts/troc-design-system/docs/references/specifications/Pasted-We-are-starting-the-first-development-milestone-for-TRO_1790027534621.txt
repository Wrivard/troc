We are starting the first development milestone for TROC.

TROC is a Canada-first trading card marketplace. The eventual platform will support Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Riftbound, sellers, collections, Smart Cart, low-value singles, seller storefronts, and more.

DO NOT BUILD THE MARKETPLACE YET.

For this milestone, your only objective is to establish the complete TROC visual identity and reusable design system that the rest of the platform will be built from.

==================================================
1. READ THE PROVIDED FILES FIRST
==================================================

Before writing code:

1. Read EVERY file included in the supplied TROC style-guide folder.
2. Inspect EVERY supplied brand/reference image.
3. Treat the written specifications as authoritative.
4. Treat the supplied TROC logo as the brand direction.
5. Do not independently redesign the TROC brand.
6. Do not start building marketplace pages, authentication, databases, checkout, seller dashboards, or backend functionality.

Before implementation, briefly summarize:
- your understanding of the TROC brand;
- the design-system architecture you will use;
- the component structure you plan to create;
- the theme/i18n approach;
- the files/routes you intend to create.

Then proceed with implementation.

==================================================
3. LOCKED VISUAL DIRECTION
==================================================

The initial/default visual identity is:

BLACK / CHARCOAL
WHITE / OFF-WHITE
GREY
TROC RED

No green, blue, purple, or other accent themes in this milestone.

Cards themselves will eventually provide much of the colour on marketplace pages.

Red should be used intentionally for:
- major CTAs
- selected states
- active navigation
- important highlights
- key brand moments
- subtle branded details

Do NOT make the entire interface red.

The overall feeling should be closer to a premium modern technology/marketplace company than a traditional collectible-card store.

Use generous whitespace and strong hierarchy.

4. LOGO
==================================================

Use the supplied TROC logo/reference files.

The selected direction is:

- strong italic / forward-moving TROC wordmark
- white on dark by default
- black version for light backgrounds
- custom red maple-leaf symbol beside the wordmark

IMPORTANT:

The custom maple leaf is a TROC brand symbol.

Do NOT replace it with:
- an emoji
- the Canadian flag emoji
- a generic icon-library maple leaf
- a randomly generated leaf
- the official Canadian flag leaf

If the supplied logo is raster/reference quality rather than a final vector asset, use it faithfully for this milestone and structure the Logo component so the final SVG can be swapped later without changing layouts.

Do not recreate the TROC wordmark using a normal font.

==================================================
10. UX PRINCIPLES
==================================================

TROC should prioritize clarity over decoration.

Do not overuse:

- cards inside cards
- huge rounded corners
- excessive shadows
- gradients
- animations
- decorative illustrations
- giant empty hero sections
- glass effects

The design must eventually handle a very information-dense marketplace without feeling cluttered.

Default toward:

clean
compact
structured
fast
easy to scan

while still feeling premium.
